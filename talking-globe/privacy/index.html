Talking Globe Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Talking Globe does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking.

What happens on your device

Place names are spoken by your device's built-in voice. Nothing you tap is recorded or sent anywhere.

Apple Maps

The globe is drawn with Apple's MapKit, which loads map imagery from Apple. Apple's handling of that request is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Talking Globe does not send Apple any information about you.

Children

Talking Globe is made for kids. Because it collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.
